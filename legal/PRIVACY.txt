LOCAL PROCESSING
Your videos and images stay on your computer. Yui has no sign-in, account connections, analytics, advertising, tracking or automatic updates. It does not access credentials or browser profiles. Media processing uses local files; network shares and network references inside media are blocked.

Original files are kept. Exported GIFs omit source metadata; visible content remains. Anonymous GIF filenames are enabled by default, so source filenames are not reused. You can choose a name with Save as or disable anonymous names in Settings. Conversion uses a checked local temporary folder and removes working files when it finishes, fails or is cancelled. GIFs go to your chosen folder, or by default to %LOCALAPPDATA%\YuiGifConverter\GIFs.

SAVED ON THIS COMPUTER
Inside %APPDATA%\YuiGifConverter:
• settings.json — options, appearance, output folder and accepted terms version.
• presets.json — saved names and conversion options; no media or file paths.
• error.log — exception types and code locations if an error occurs; new entries omit media names, full paths and exception messages.

Delete that folder to remove preferences, presets and logs. Your exported GIFs are kept separately. Delete the program file to remove the app.

OPTIONAL ACTIONS
• Ko-fi: clicking Support Yui opens https://ko-fi.com/yui_xxx in your browser. Yui receives no account or payment details.
• Yui on JuicyChat: opens the creator profile in your browser when clicked. Its profile picture is bundled locally; the app does not sign in or connect to a JuicyChat account.
• Copy GIF: copies a file to the Windows clipboard when you choose it, with Windows clipboard-history and cloud-sync exclusion flags. Other clipboard tools may follow their own rules.
• Send to: adds or removes the Explorer shortcut when you change the setting.

Cloud-sync software may sync folders you choose. External websites and other applications manage their own accounts and privacy. Yui does not connect to those accounts. The Ko-fi and JuicyChat links and profile picture are intentional public creator branding.
